# Privacy Policy: Moonlight store app (Meta)

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy covers the Meta app "Moonlight Autopilot" (App ID 1414532210811508), which connects the Moonlight shop's own Facebook Page and Instagram account (@moonlight_14a) to the shop's order and customer-service system. It is a temporary page used while the shop's website is not yet online; the shop's website will publish its full privacy policy at launch.

## What the app accesses

Only for the Facebook Page and Instagram account the shop's owner connects:

- the Page's and account's name and ID;
- messages and comments people send to the shop's Page or Instagram account, with the sender's public name or username and ID;
- the shop's own posts and their statistics (views, reach, likes, saves);
- the permission to reply to messages and comments and to publish the shop's posts, always after a member of the shop's staff approves.

The app does not access anyone's password, private profile, friends, or any account that has not been connected by its owner.

## Why

To let the shop's staff read and answer customer questions and comments in one place, plan and publish the shop's own posts, and see how those posts perform. AI may draft a reply; a person reviews every reply before it is sent.

## Storage and security

Data is stored on the shop's own servers. Access keys are encrypted. Each shop's data is kept separate from any other shop's. Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build profiles of people.

## How long

Messages and comments are kept while needed for customer service and the shop's records. When the shop disconnects its Page, the stored access key and the notifications received from Meta are deleted.

## Deleting your data

To ask for deletion of the messages or comments you sent to the shop, send a direct message to **@moonlight_14a** on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We delete them within 30 days and confirm by message.

You can also remove the app's access to your own Facebook or Instagram business account at any time in Facebook: **Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations**, or in Instagram: **Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites**.

## Contact

Send a direct message to **@moonlight_14a** on Instagram.
